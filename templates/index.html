Automatic mode

You don't pick an algorithm. Each waiting patient is put in a queue by how serious they are, and each queue uses the algorithm that suits it:

Critical P1–P2Priority with aging
Urgent P3Shortest Job First
Routine P4–P5First Come First Served

Critical is always served first, then Urgent, then Routine. A patient who waits too long is aged up into a higher queue, so nobody waits forever. Then the ranking module picks the hospital and the transaction manager reserves the unit.
